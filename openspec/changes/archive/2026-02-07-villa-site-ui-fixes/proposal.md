## Why

The site’s current UI polish falls short of the intended warm, nature-forward experience, with readability and navigation issues across pages. Addressing these gaps now will improve perceived quality and usability before wider sharing.

## What Changes

- Improve navigation contrast and main color accents, including olive highlights and subpage styling.
- Make the navbar responsive with a mobile-friendly layout.
- Remove unintended horizontal/side scroll visibility and ensure smooth, natural scrolling.
- Add spacing between the hero and subsequent sections for clearer content separation.
- Update typography to Lato across the site to match the desired tone.
- Introduce more readable olive accents via captions and supporting text.
- Add social media icons above the contact form.
