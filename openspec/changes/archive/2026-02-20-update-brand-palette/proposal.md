## Why

The new client logo color should feel integrated with the overall site palette while keeping the website elegant, readable, and WCAG-friendly. Updating the palette now ensures branding consistency and avoids mismatched accents across navigation, buttons, and section highlights.

## What Changes

- Introduce a logo-aligned brand tint and adjust accent colors to maintain contrast.
- Keep warm neutrals for a luxurious, welcoming villa feel.
- Define usage rules so brand tint is used for large branding, and accents remain accessible.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `brand-palette-refresh`: A cohesive, accessible color palette that aligns with the new logo tint and defines usage roles for brand, accent, and neutral colors.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- Global CSS variables and theme tokens.
- Navbar brand styling and accent usage across pages.
