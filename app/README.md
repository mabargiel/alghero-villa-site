# Alghero Villa Site

Marketing website for Villa Monte Calvia — a private house for rent in Sardinia,
highlighting the property, garden, and guest experience.

## Technologies

- [Next.js](https://nextjs.org) for the web app
- [Sanity](https://www.sanity.io) for content management
- [Resend](https://resend.com) for email delivery
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com) for hosting and deployments
- [OpenSpec](https://openspec.dev) for product and design specifications
